Time is a one-way street
every mark on the dial exact
no need to think, no need to doubt
red means stop
green means go
jumping into the sea is only a game
you mustn't let yourself sink

A boat no bigger than a leaf
oars pulling, sail full
far away is only ever up ahead

But in the vast maze of neurons and synapses
that moment had no equal
the Marlboro slipped out through a crack in the window
the cabernet hid in his arms

Cards on the table
one moment traded for a lifetime
the biggest winner
no more fear of what a long night brings
"The deal of the century!"

But after all the circling
how could that moment ever be bought back?
