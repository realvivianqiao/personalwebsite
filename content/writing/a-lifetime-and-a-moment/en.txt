Time is a one-way street
every notch on it exact
no need to think, no need to doubt
red means stop
green means go
jumping into the sea is a game
don't let yourself go under

A boat no bigger than a leaf
rowing, sail full
the far place is always up ahead

But in that vast maze of neurons and synapses
there was nothing like that moment
the Marlboro slipping out through the crack in the window
the cabernet hiding in his arms

Cards face up
a moment traded for a lifetime
the biggest winner at the table
no more fear of the long night
"The deal of the century!"

But for all the going round and round
how do you ever buy that moment back?
