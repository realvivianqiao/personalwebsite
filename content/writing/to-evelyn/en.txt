This is one of the very first autumn nights in New York this year, should've been cold, should've been rain's grace. Yet oddly warm, the winds do sway. As his hoarse voice sings in the back. The room is in disarray — unlit candles, tables frail, they bear my strain. But still I wanna write a poem for Evelyn. Evelyn is fictional. Evelyn only exists in my head. Evelyn is the epitome of every single thing that would've left a mark on my poetic memory.

You told me that love is a symphony of souls.

But confusion gripped my soul, a tangled thorn. In shadows cast by love's relentless storm, I broke, I bled. I didn't flee, though my heart quaked. You said the true meaning of love, a quest profound. To seek it out, we should journey far and wide, In every tear and every scar we bide.

But should we pursue a purpose told? Or venture where no other has dared to hold? In every fleeting moment, I seeked your answer. I would've leaped off the ship. I would've swirled. I would've stayed.

But Evelyn, you left me in the quest alone. You never embarked. And now I'm here in this pain, grasping my chance to be reborn.
