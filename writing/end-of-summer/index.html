夏末随感

End of Summer

End of Summer

夏末随感

3 min read约 3 分钟First published on WeChat ↗首发于微信公众号 ↗

不知不觉夏天又到了尽头。北方季节的轮回，无论在哪里，好像都是场流动的盛宴：夏天永远是high note，然后是渐渐淡下去的秋天，寂寥的冬天，和万物复苏的春天。在北京的时候恨极了那里干枯的秋冬，想着之后一定要生活在个四季如春、阳光明媚的地方；结果现在窗外雷鸣电闪，秋风瑟瑟，雨点儿在黑色的水面上溅起涟漪；空气一点点的冷下去，人们换上trench coat和严肃表情，叶子开始掉光。季节的更替、万物的轮回、世事的变迁，对于整个世界来说是多么正常，多么微不足道、不值一提的一件事情。可是看到这里的每个人，还是可以清晰地感觉到，夏天确实结束了，那些因为是夏天而变得legit的浪漫和快乐，好像就不应该那么无忧无虑地继续了。

人们愿意给每个季节赋予意义，又愿意给每个意义找到支撑它的理由。秋天是"古道西风瘦马"，是南瓜枫叶橙黄色；冬天是"千山鸟飞绝"，是梅花白雪和深夜的酒巷子；夏天是Calvin Harris的Summer，是大丽花海滩和吊带裙。人们愿意给每一段记忆赋予一首歌、一幅画，又愿意赋予它对应的季节。好像夏天就应该是summer fling，秋天就是分别，冬天是缅怀，春天又是新的开始。以至于到了以后的以后，听到这首歌，看到这幅画，甚至只是到了这个季节，好像都还是会想起对应的人和事，和记忆中点点滴滴残缺的感觉。

小时候常常在想，究竟是人生如戏，还是戏如人生？是先人为我们的生活制定规则然后我们follow，还是我们自己摸索然后给自己的人生赋予意义？现在所有人都可以跳出来分享自己的人生经验，告诉别人自己曾经怎么做，所以你应该怎么做。好像人生确实有严丝密封的规则，你只要照着做，就能获得规则里规定的成功。要好好读书，要有体面的工作，要礼貌得体，要有精致的皮囊，要有爱自己的伴侣。一个box没有被check，then there is something missing。可是人生的意义究竟是停顿的那一刹那，还是走过一路的点点滴滴？人生又该怎么应该活出自己想要的样子，同时又不被条条框框和世俗的规定束缚？

如果没有了这些世俗的束缚，是不是人生才可以真正的得以自由。真正的知道，自己喜欢的东西、自己爱的人、自己想做的事情，会是什么样子的；而不是低头看看世俗的参照板，然后点点头告诉自己，这是个让人满意、让人羡慕、不会让人失望的选择，然后这就足够了。

人生越是往前走，反而越希望自己能允许自己犯错，允许自己做蠢事，甚至允许自己被伤害。允许自己不再把执着的把自己的人生当成一张白白的纸，而是一副绚丽的画布。只是多了几笔浓墨重彩，而不是毁了原来的洁白无瑕。

纽约的秋天听说很快就会过去，再就又是寒冷光秃秃的冬天。街上开始有穿皮裤和高筒靴风度翩翩的女孩子，和穿风衣皮鞋戴围巾行色匆匆的男生。河边的水开始变冷，郁郁葱葱的树开始变光。可是纵使季节再萧瑟，生活不是也还可以继续潇洒。

Summer is over again before I noticed. In the north, wherever you are, the seasons turn like a moveable feast: summer is always the high note, then autumn slowly fading, then lonely winter, then spring when everything comes back. In Beijing I hated those dry autumns and winters so much I swore I'd live somewhere sunny, where it's spring all year. And here I am with thunder and lightning outside the window, a shivering autumn wind, rain pocking the black water. The air gets colder day by day, people put on trench coats and serious faces, and the trees start losing their leaves. The seasons changing, everything coming round again, the world moving on: to the world, nothing could be more normal or less worth mentioning. And yet you can see it in everyone here. Summer really is over, and the romance and happiness that summer made legit can't go on quite so carefree anymore.

People like to give every season a meaning, and then find reasons to hold that meaning up. Autumn is "an old road, the west wind, a thin horse," pumpkins and maple leaves and orange. Winter is "a thousand mountains, not a single bird," plum blossom and white snow and the bars down little lanes late at night. Summer is Calvin Harris's "Summer," dahlias and beaches and slip dresses. We like to give every memory a song or a painting, and then give it the season that goes with it, as if summer were meant for a fling, autumn for goodbyes, winter for remembering, and spring for starting over. So that long afterward, hearing the song, seeing the painting, or just feeling the season come round, you still think of the people and the things that go with it, and all the broken pieces of feeling left in the memory.

When I was little I used to wonder whether life is like a play or a play is like life. Did the people before us write the rules for our lives so we could follow them, or do we feel our way and give our lives meaning ourselves? Now anyone can jump up and share their life experience, tell you what they did and therefore what you should do. As if life really did run on airtight rules, and if you followed them you'd get the success they promise. Study hard, get a respectable job, be polite and proper, be polished on the outside, find a partner who loves you. If one box doesn't get checked, then there's something missing. But is the meaning of life that one pause, or everything along the way? And how do you live the life you want without being boxed in by rules and the world's expectations?

Maybe only without those expectations can a life really be free. You'd really know what the things you like, the people you love, the things you want to do look like, instead of glancing at the world's chart, nodding, and telling yourself this is a choice that satisfies, impresses, disappoints no one, and that's enough.

The further I go, the more I want to let myself make mistakes, do stupid things, even get hurt. To stop treating my life as a clean sheet of white paper and start treating it as a vivid canvas, where a few more heavy strokes of color are only more color, not the ruin of something perfect.

People say autumn in New York is over fast, and then it's cold, bare winter again. The girls on the street have started wearing leather pants and tall boots, very elegant, and the boys hurry by in trench coats and leather shoes and scarves. The river is getting cold, and the green trees are going bare. But however bleak the season, can't life still go on with a little flair?
